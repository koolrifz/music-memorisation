# Beat Smash (working title): feel the beat before reading a note

**The full brief is private:** `kool-riffs-docs/docs/beat-smash-design-brief.md`
(draft 2). This page is the one-page version, as `ideas/README.md` asks.

## What it teaches

The very first contact with rhythm. Rob, 2026-09-28 and 29:

> *"They see four squares horizontally… each represent one beat, and they're
> synchronised with audio."*

> *"Press and hold for sustain… you literally make them play a half note."*

> *"Any interaction with beat should definitely look like an MPC, those kind of
> beat pads."*

> *"At the end of that level they need to be reading notation, so this is
> definitely a means to an end."*

An MPC: a drum-machine display on top, glowing pads below. The display shows
coloured blocks on a row of beat cells (a quarter note fills one cell, a half
note two, a whole note four; rests are the same blocks, hollow). The student
taps a pad for a note, **holds** it for a long note, and leaves it alone for a
rest, in time with music they choose. Views switch between blocks, dice, a drum
machine and notation. **The floor is cleared only by playing 4 and 8 bars of
real notation on the pad**; the blocks stay open as a playground.

Beat Blocks – Rhythm Practice is Rob's reference for the direction, not a
design to copy.

## Why the app misses it

Value Smash teaches what a note is *worth*; Stomp Lab teaches how to *count and
write* it. Nothing teaches what a beat *feels* like before a symbol arrives.
Garnet's *"what I need is a step before this"* is the evidence.

## Floors (draft)

0 Find the Pulse · 1 Beat and Hush (quarter + rest) · 2 The Hold (half + rest)
· 3 Beat Meets Hold · 4 The Long One (whole + rest, then all) · 5 The Reveal,
then The Gig (notation on the pad) · ★ Blackout. Each floor grows 1 → 2 → 4 → 8
bars, and three clean phrases in a row moves the student up a tier. Twelve game
ideas (Read & Play, Copycat, Roll the Dice, The Roll, Beat Builder, …) are in
the brief's §7.

## Not decided

- Dice: pips for how the beat divides, or pips for the beat numbers.
- Colours (proposed: quarter blue, half orange, whole purple; rests the same
  colour, hollow).
- Accent on a separate pad (a phone can't sense how hard you tap).
- Two half notes in one bar (Stomp Lab's A2 excludes it).
- **The Artistic License**: Rob now defines it as clearing all the pillars,
  which conflicts with Value Smash V1 awarding it. Not being built now.
- Whether it gates Value Smash, and the name ("Kool Beat" is the metronome).
