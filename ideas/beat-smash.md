# Beat Smash (working title): feel the beat before reading a note

**The full brief is private:** `kool-riffs-docs/docs/beat-smash-design-brief.md`.
This page is the one-page version, as `ideas/README.md` asks.

## What it teaches

The very first contact with rhythm. Rob, 2026-09-28:

> *"They see four squares horizontally, one after another. Those four squares
> each represent one beat, and they're synchronised with audio… The first thing
> they've got to do is just interact with that beat."*

> *"Press and hold for sustain… you literally make them play a half note."*

A row of beat cells, with coloured blocks laid over them (a quarter note fills
one cell, a half note two, a whole note four; rests are the same blocks,
hollow). The student taps a big pad for a note, **holds** it for a long note,
and leaves it alone for a rest, in time with music they choose. No notation
until the last floor, where the blocks turn into Stomp Lab's staff.

## Why the app misses it

Value Smash teaches what a note is *worth*; Stomp Lab teaches how to *count and
write* it. Nothing teaches what a beat *feels* like before a symbol arrives.
Garnet's *"what I need is a step before this"* is the evidence.

## Floors (draft)

0 Find the Pulse · 1 Beat and Hush (quarter + rest) · 2 The Hold (half + rest)
· 3 Beat Meets Hold · 4 The Long One (whole + rest, then all) · 5 The Reveal
(blocks → notation) · ★ Maestro Bonus: Blackout. Each floor grows 1 → 2 → 4 →
8 bars, and three clean phrases in a row moves the student up a tier.

## Not decided

- Symbols riding along inside the blocks from floor 3, or none until floor 5.
- Colours (proposed: quarter blue, half orange, whole purple; rests the same
  colour but hollow).
- Two half notes in one bar (Stomp Lab's A2 excludes it).
- A sustained instrument sound under the thumb, or drums.
- Whether clearing it gates Value Smash.
- The name ("Kool Beat" is the metronome).
