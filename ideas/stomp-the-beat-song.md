# Stomp the Beat: a recorded song with studio punch-in hurdles

**Status:** prototype built, 2026-10-23, branch `idea/stomp-the-beat`. Needs a
phone playtest by Rob (timing feel, guide kick level, page turning).

## In Rob's words

> *"Don't drop out the band. Drop the guide track for 4 beats. Treat it like a
> recording studio. We're going to drop them in for four beats. And we do
> exactly the same thing for 2, 4, 8, 32 bars of crotchets. Then we start
> another round and include crotchet rests. Give the option to play with the
> guide track which plays the written rhythms."*

> *"For a prototype let's hook this up as a song on the studio with our hurdles
> to pass."*

## What it is

A Suno-made song (Rob's remaster, 172 s, about 111 to 118 bpm) played from its
RECORDING as a tile on Beat Smash's song chooser. Free play (stomp along, a
summary of where the taps fell), and two rounds of hurdles: 1, 2, 4, 8 bars,
then a 32-bar recording session (silver, then gold record), first of crotchets,
then of crotchets and crotchet rests (opens once a record is pressed).

## How it is built

- `content/<id>-beats.js`: a beat map (329 per-beat times), generated by
  `tools/build-stomp-chart.py` from the audio (librosa + low-band kick snap +
  robust spline). Kick match median 1.8 ms. Timing is against the MAP, never a
  fixed tempo, because the track speeds up. Re-run the tool for a new song.
- `content/stomp-songs.js`: sections (Rob's written form, bar 0 = count-in)
  and the hurdles with the bars each attempt may be taken from.
- `stomp-song-core.js`: pure planning, grading, progress (`tools/test-stomp-song.js`).
- `stomp-song.js`: screens and audio. `tools/test-stomp-song.py` plays a hurdle.
- Grading reuses `KRStomp.createMatcher` (stomp-round.js).

## Decisions (and what was rejected)

- Band never drops out; only the GUIDE kick does. Rejected: a kick-free
  regeneration; tempo-warping to a fixed grid (invasive to Beat Smash).
- Guide kick = the student's kick, filtered round and quieter, so the student's
  is clearly the head one.
- GUIDE button ON plays the written rhythm in the gap; that take is practice
  and does not count (own call; Rob to confirm).
- Taps on a rest are mistakes (own call). Window 0.18 s, no age tables.
- Pass marks: 100% for 1 and 2 bars, 85% for the longer ones. Rule of three.
- A tap more than half a beat past the double bar line spoils the take.
