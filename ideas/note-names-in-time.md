# Note names in time (Zach's idea)

**Status:** an idea, 2026-10-08. Nothing built. From Zach, in Rob's grade 5
class, after playing Beat Smash.

## In Rob's words

> *"One of the boys, Zach, in my grade five class, he just loved it. And he
> said, I need to make one where I have to tap the appropriate letter name in
> time. So one step above what we're doing here, instead of four buttons, you
> would have seven or eight, like... the top of the Note Smash ones, the last
> game of that... So you're doing it in time... I could imagine a really
> simple game of half notes and quarter notes and you're tapping the note
> name in time. So I thought that was pretty fun. So we can workshop that
> idea and put it on a list."*

## What it would be

Pitch and rhythm together: a short line of half and quarter notes on a staff,
over a groove, and the student taps each note's **letter name** on a row of
seven (or eight) keys **in time**, as Beat Smash taps the beat. Real Smash's
answer keys, Beat Smash's clock and grading.

It is the first game where the two silos meet in one action, which is what
Riff and Tango stand for: *"Together rhythm and pitch make melody, and that's
music."*

## Not decided

- Where it sits: after Real Smash, after Beat Smash, or as a bridge level
  between them (see "SILOS AND BRIDGES" in `CLAUDE.md`).
- Seven keys (letters) or eight (an octave, so the line has a direction).
- Which clef and range first; whether the keys look like a piano or Real
  Smash's thumb pad.
- How a right letter at the wrong time is marked (the pad-is-the-beat rule's
  equivalent).
