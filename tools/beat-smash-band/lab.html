Bass and keys ring for as long as you hold. With the band playing, they follow the chord of the bar it's on; stopped, they play chord I. Space bar works too.

The rendered files are in audio/beat-smash/ in the app repo, named as the spec asks (beat-l1-drums-spicy.wav and so on). This page plays the same code that rendered them.

Spicy is Latin (bossa kick, son clave, congas, upright bass, montuno keys). Smooth is jazz played straight (ride, cross-stick, electric bass, suitcase Rhodes). Hop is dance (four on the floor, off-beat synth bass, plucks over a pumping pad).
